### C2 Concurrent starts on one Group race
- verdict: CONFIRMED
- severity: medium
- location: crates/bridge/src/app_api.rs:332-353 (store-sqlite lib.rs:427-447)
- problem: Two starts on one Group interleave as store A, store B (deletes A's row), play B, play A. Group ends up playing A's URL; A's stream id is gone, B is the listed Handoff. Reachable: no per-Group or global lock around start_handoff + sonos.play.
- evidence: main.rs:51 plain `#[tokio::main]` (multi-thread; interleaving possible even single-thread since both awaits yield). app_api.rs has no Mutex/Semaphore. Store Mutex<Connection> only wraps each spawn_blocking transaction (one per call), not the store+play pair; start_handoff does `DELETE FROM handoffs WHERE group_id=?1` then INSERT. sonos upnp.rs play (294-345) takes no per-Group lock (only a brief std Mutex on `known`, not held across await); it does topology read, SetAVTransportURI, Play, so two plays on one coordinator can complete in either order. stream.rs:70-76 unknown stream id -> not_found(). Outcome: speaker fetches A's URL -> 404 -> playback fails/stops (or stays silent), while the app lists B as "playing on" the Group; both callers got 200. Window = Sonos round trip (hundreds of ms), needs two users/devices starting on the same Group at nearly the same time; the last-store-wins on the same Group is otherwise by design (replace).
- fix: serialize start per Group (per-Group async mutex across store+play), or after play re-check that the stream is still the Group's current Handoff and otherwise return a conflict/replaced error.
- confidence: high
