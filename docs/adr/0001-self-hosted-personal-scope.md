# Self-hosted, build-it-yourself scope; no App Store or Sonos partner track

Shelfcast is a personal and portfolio project for one household. It is released as open source, and anyone else who wants it builds and installs it themselves. We are not designing for App Store review (guideline 4.2) or Sonos partner certification, so we may use free Sonos developer access, unsupported local Sonos interfaces, and sideloaded iOS builds. Sonos approvals or fees are ruled out.
